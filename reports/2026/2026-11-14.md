JPL Sentry lists 2250 objects and ESA NEOCC lists 2210 (ESA list updated 2026-11-14 00:20 UTC). 1980 are on both lists, 270 only on JPL's and 230 only on ESA's. For 300 of the objects on both lists, one agency's impact probability is at least 10 times the other's, and the Torino scale differs for 0.

A difference is not by itself an error. The two centres compute their orbits independently, from observations up to different times, and list potential impacts over different time spans (the Years columns), so the same object can get different numbers, or appear on one list only.
